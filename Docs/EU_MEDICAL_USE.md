# EU medical use and regulatory status

## Status of version 0.8

Liver IronLoad 0.8 is released for software research, education and comparison
of quantitative MRI implementations. It is not released for patient diagnosis
or treatment decisions. Clinical validation, MDR conformity assessment and
CE marking have not been completed for this plugin. This statement concerns
this implementation, independently of the status of IsiX or MRQuantif.

## Regulation (EU) 2017/745 on medical devices (MDR)

Software with a medical intended purpose can fall within the MDR. Intended
purpose and product claims matter; a disclaimer alone does not settle
qualification. Open-source licensing and a zero price do not establish an
exemption. Article 5 addresses putting devices into service and conformity.

Annex VIII, Rule 11 addresses software providing information for diagnostic
or therapeutic decisions. Such software is generally Class IIa, with higher
classes for specified consequences. No final classification is assigned here.
Before any clinical release, the responsible manufacturer must determine the
applicable obligations, including risk management, technical documentation,
clinical evaluation, conformity assessment and post-market requirements.

Article 5(5) provides a conditional health-institution route. It is not a
general exemption for private practice or a public GitHub release; its
conditions require separate assessment. Local comparison with another tool
alone does not establish regulatory compliance.

The MIT License governs copyright permissions, not authorization for medical
use. Nothing in this repository certifies conformity or replaces qualified
regulatory advice for the intended deployment in Germany or elsewhere.
