# Acknowledgements

We gratefully acknowledge **Professor Yves Gandon, Rennes, France**, and his
collaborators for their influential work on MRI-based quantification of liver
iron and fat, and for developing **MRQuantif**, previously known as IronByMR.
Their publications, practical acquisition protocols and accessible software
have made an important contribution to the clinical and scientific community.

MRQuantif inspired this project's ROI workflow and its goal of making
quantitative liver MRI easier to access within a DICOM viewer. Readers are
encouraged to consult the original work and to cite its authors when using
their methods:

- [MRQuantif website](https://radsapp.net/mrquantif)
- [MRQuantif author and software information](https://radsapp.net/mrquantif/about.html)
- [Original protocols](https://radsapp.net/mrquantif/protocols)

Liver IronLoad is an independent Swift implementation. Prof. Gandon and the
MRQuantif team have not reviewed, endorsed or certified this plugin. Their
names are used for scientific acknowledgement, not to suggest affiliation or
to transfer rights in their software. MRQuantif code, binaries, screenshots
and documentation are not included or relicensed by this project.

We also acknowledge **Diego Hernando and colleagues** for the published
field-strength-dependent iron calibration used as an exploratory comparison,
and the other researchers cited in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md). Their
validation results do not automatically apply to this implementation.

Thanks to **Thales Matheus M Santos (ThalesMMS)** for IsiX DICOM Viewer,
IsiXPluginKit, the plugin template and the 4D viewer API introduced in IsiX
6.0.8. We acknowledge the Horos and OsiriX contributors behind the host's
history. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
